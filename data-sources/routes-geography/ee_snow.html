Page Summary

  • Climate and cryosphere datasets are available, including reanalysis products, forecasts, and snow and glacier measurements.

  • Datasets cover various temporal resolutions, from daily to monthly and 8-day composites.

  • Geographical coverage varies across datasets, including global, Continental North America, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico.

  • Data is provided by organizations such as NOAA, NCEP, ECMWF, and NASA, derived from satellite imagery and meteorological stations.

  • Variables included in these datasets encompass snow cover, glacier properties, atmospheric variables, and land surface parameters.
